## Research Notes

### Fan Dipole
Pros:
- Multiband
- Lower cost
- Easier installation
- Good DX capability

Cons:
- Larger wire spread
- Possible interaction between elements

### Yagi
Pros:
- Directional gain
- Better signal strength

Cons:
- Expensive
- Requires rotor/tower
- More complex mechanically

---

## Current Decision
Currently leaning toward a multiband fan dipole in an inverted-V configuration for communication with Brazil on 10m, 15m, and 20m.
