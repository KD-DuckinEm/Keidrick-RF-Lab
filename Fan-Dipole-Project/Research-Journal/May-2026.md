## Research Notes

### Fan Dipole
Pros:
- Multiband
- Lower cost
- Easier installation
- Good DX capability

Cons:
- Larger wire spread
- Possible interaction between elements

### Yagi
Pros:
- Directional gain
- Better signal strength

Cons:
- Expensive
- Requires rotor/tower
- More complex mechanically

## Research Completed

### Antenna Types Investigated
- Yagi
- Monopole
- Dipole
- Inverted-V dipole
- Fan dipole

### Current Conclusion
A fan dipole appears to best fit the project goals because:
- multiband capability
- lower cost
- simpler mechanical setup
- good DX potential

Yagi antennas were considered but currently seem less practical due to:
- size
- complexity
- cost
- directional requirements
